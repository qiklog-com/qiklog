The QikLog mark

A prompt frame with the corner broken open, a chevron inside, and the tail crossing the gap. Reads as a terminal prompt and as a Q. Spec lives in docs/BRAND.md.

Small sizes

Strokes are 8 units on a 64 grid so the counter stays open and the chevron stays legible down to favicon scale.
